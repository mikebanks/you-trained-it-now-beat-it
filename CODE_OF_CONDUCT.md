# Code of conduct

This is a learning resource for teachers, students, parents, and contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people with respect, explain feedback constructively, and make room for questions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual content directed at participants, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Keep examples fictional and do not post student information or images.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, and other project discussions. Maintainers may remove inappropriate material, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Concerns may be reported privately to Michael Banks through https://www.michaelbanks.org/contact. Maintainers will consider context and respond proportionately.
